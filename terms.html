Terms of Service – BasketNews Dashboard

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. About this app

BasketNews Dashboard (“the app”) is an internal reporting tool operated by BasketNews. It reads performance statistics of BasketNews’s own TikTok accounts so our editorial team can review which videos performed best each week.

2. Who may use it

The app is for use by BasketNews staff only. It is not offered to the public, and only TikTok accounts owned or managed by BasketNews are connected to it.

3. What the app does

With the account owner’s permission, the app reads basic profile information and the list of public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts. It does not post, edit, delete or message on TikTok.

4. Disconnecting

An account owner can revoke the app’s access at any time in TikTok’s settings under Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

5. No warranty

The app is provided as is, for internal reporting purposes, without warranties of any kind.
