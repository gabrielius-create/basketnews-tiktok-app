Privacy Policy – BasketNews Dashboard

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

BasketNews Dashboard (“the app”) is an internal reporting tool operated by BasketNews. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

2. Data we access

Only for TikTok accounts owned or managed by BasketNews, and only after the account owner authorizes the app, we access:

  • basic profile information (account ID and display name);
  • the list of the account’s public videos: video ID, link, description, publish time, and view, like, comment and share counts.

We do not access private messages, followers’ personal data, or any data of other TikTok users.

3. How we use it

The data is used solely to produce internal weekly performance reports for the BasketNews editorial team. It is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for advertising.

4. Where it is stored

Video statistics are stored in a private Google Sheet belonging to BasketNews, accessible only to BasketNews staff. Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored privately within the app’s Google Apps Script project.

5. Retention

Statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for reporting. Access tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected.

6. Revoking access

An account owner can revoke the app’s access at any time in TikTok’s settings under Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. After that, the app can no longer read new data.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests: social@basketnews.com
